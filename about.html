About BrickPix

Transform your favorite photos into stunning Lego mosaics with our innovative app

What is BrickPix?

BrickPix is an innovative application designed to turn your cherished photos into beautiful Lego mosaics. Whether you want to create a portrait of a loved one, capture a favorite memory, or bring any image to life with Lego bricks, BrickPix makes the creative process simple, intuitive, and enjoyable.

Our app uses advanced image processing technology to analyze your photos and generate precise, color-coded building guides that include detailed piece lists and step-by-step instructions. No artistic experience required – just upload, customize, and build!

How It Works

Creating a Lego mosaic with BrickPix is as easy as 1-2-3-4:

1

Upload Your Photo

Choose any image from your device and upload it to the BrickPix app

2

Customize Colors

3

Generate Guide

4

Build & Enjoy

Follow the step-by-step guide and piece list to build your masterpiece

The Magic Behind BrickPix

At the heart of BrickPix is sophisticated image analysis technology that:

  • Analyzes pixel data to identify colors in your image
  • Maps colors to the closest available Lego brick colors
  • Generates an optimal grid layout for your mosaic
  • Creates detailed building instructions with clear piece counts
  • Optimizes the design for visual impact and buildability

This allows us to transform any photograph into a buildable Lego mosaic while maintaining the essence and visual quality of your original image.
